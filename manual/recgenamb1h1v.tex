First order Ambisonics encoder, B-format (FuMa channel sequence
``wxyz'').

\begin{lstlisting}[numbers=none]
<receiver type="amb1h1v"/>
\end{lstlisting}

The normalization attributes \attr{normalization="FuMa"} (default) or
\attr{normalization="SN3D"} are supported.

\input{tabreceiveramb1h1v.tex}

For compatibility with AmbiX format chose \attr{channelorder="ACN"} and \attr{normalization="SN3D"}.
